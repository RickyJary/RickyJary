## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a web developer based in Madrid with a background in technology, administration, music, and team management.

My route into tech was not exactly conventional. Before writing code, I spent more than ten years working as a DJ and music producer, performing across Europe and managing a long-term creative project alongside other people.

That experience taught me how to work under pressure, adapt when plans change, communicate with very different people, and take an idea from its first stages to a finished result.

After completing Ironhack's Web Development Bootcamp, I began building full-stack applications with **React, Node.js, Express, and MongoDB**.

I particularly enjoy projects where creativity and logic meet: practical digital tools, interactive interfaces, gaming experiences, and products that solve real problems.

* 🔭 Building and improving personal web projects
* 📊 Expanding my knowledge of Excel, Power BI, and data analysis
* 🐧 Learning more about Linux, Docker, networks, and deployment
* 🎮 Interested in gaming and interactive digital experiences
* 🎵 Former DJ and music producer with more than a decade of experience
* 📍 Based in Madrid, Spain
* 🌍 Spanish, English, and French

* Backend built with Node.js and Express
* MongoDB database
* Spotify API integration
* Role-based authentication and permissions
